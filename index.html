Understand the Endpoint

Before endpoint: added NaOH reacts with oxalic acid, so the pink colour does not persist.

Near endpoint: add NaOH very slowly, one drop at a time, while swirling.

Endpoint: the solution becomes a very faint pink that persists. Do not aim for dark pink.

Calculation: for diprotic oxalic acid, use 2M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ when M₁ is oxalic acid molarity and M₂ is NaOH molarity.
